How to read this board — one decision rule, two estimates, one evidence check
Decision Gate — the gate model's predicted return on the trade against its threshold. Pass = tradeable. This is the selection rule the backtests used; the gate reads its own features and never sees the size model. A ranking, not a probability of profit.
Forecast Exp PnL (model) — the size champion's Monte-Carlo forecast for THIS trade: draws from the champion's own held-out errors, pushed through a payoff map fitted on real replayed trades, priced at the real quoted premium. Win is its probability of profit. A forecast, not a selector.
Evidence Exp PnL (analogs) — what matched historical trades actually made, priced by the same replay code; n is how many stand behind it, and the bracket on Win is their bootstrap interval.
The estimates are never averaged: opposite signs flag LAYER_DISAGREE, which means the model may be extrapolating past its evidence. The amber gate vs model pill marks rows where the gate passes while the model forecast is negative — two independent estimators disagreeing that hard is rare, so treat the row with suspicion rather than as a trade. OUT_OF_DOMAIN means the gate decision is withheld: the name was outside the champion gate's training universe (a <1B name or a name the oquants panel does not carry, both added after the gate was promoted — EXP-118), so no decision exists for it yet.
